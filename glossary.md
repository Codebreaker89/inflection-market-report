## How the System Runs

### Fully Automatic (Mon–Fri 10am)

The LaunchAgent runs `run_notify.sh` automatically. It does this in sequence:

```
python3 scan.py --no-backtest
    → Runs all 10 scanners against the full universe (US + Europe + Canada)
    → Prints results to notify.log
    → Saves last_scan.json (consumed by next two steps)

python3 update_scan_history.py
    → Appends today's flagged tickers to scan_history.csv
    → Backfills d5/d10 returns for any rows that are now 5 or 10 trading days old
    → d5/d10 columns populate automatically over time — no manual action needed

python3 notify.py --send
    → Sends the HTML email digest to your Gmail
    → Email includes: alerts (stop loss / hold expired / profit target), open positions P&L,
      cross-strategy matrix, and a link to open tracker.html

python3 notify.py
    → Saves digest_preview.html locally (same email, no send — open in browser)
```

### Trigger Manually (any time)

If you want to run it now without waiting for the schedule:

```bash
# Trigger via LaunchAgent (runs run_notify.sh, logs to notify.log)
launchctl kickstart gui/$(id -u)/com.hemant.tradedigest

# Or run directly in terminal to see live output
cd ~/Claude/Projects/fire && python3 scan.py --no-backtest && python3 update_scan_history.py
```

### Monitor What's Happening

```bash
tail -f ~/Claude/Projects/fire/notify.log
```

### Open Results After a Run

```bash
open ~/Claude/Projects/fire/tracker.html        # portfolio: P&L, stop loss, hold days
open ~/Claude/Projects/fire/digest_preview.html # email preview: alerts + scan matrix
open ~/Claude/Projects/fire/scan_history.csv    # historical dataset
```

### How Historical Data Builds Up

- **Day 0 (today):** scan runs → ticker appended to `scan_history.csv` with `price_at_scan`; d5/d10 columns are blank.
- **Day 5:** next run of `update_scan_history.py` sees the row is 5 trading days old → fetches current price → fills `price_d5`, `ret_d5`, `spy_ret_d5`, `excess_ret_d5`, `hit_stop_loss_d5`.
- **Day 10:** same for d10 columns.
- Over weeks/months this builds a dataset you can query to measure which strategies actually make money.

## trades.csv Columns

### Core Trade Fields

- **id** — Auto-incrementing trade number
- **entry_date** — Date you bought the stock (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **ticker** — yfinance symbol. US stocks: no suffix (e.g. `AMAT`). UK: `.L`. Germany: `.DE`. France: `.PA`. Canada: `.TO`
- **company** — Company name, auto-fetched from yfinance
- **currency** — Local trading currency: `USD`, `GBP`, `EUR`, `CAD`, `CHF`, etc.
- **buy_price** — Price paid per share in local currency
- **fx_at_entry** — EUR→local FX rate on entry date (e.g. `1.08` means 1 EUR = 1.08 USD)
- **qty** — Shares purchased (= 1000 EUR × fx_at_entry ÷ buy_price)
- **investment_eur** — Amount invested in EUR (default €1000, can vary)
- **trade_type** — `practice` (paper trade) or `real` (actual money)
- **strategy** — which scanner flagged it: `momentum`, `breakout`, `pocket_pivot`, `connors_rsi2`, `ema_ribbon`, `nr7`, `bb_squeeze`, `high_tight_flag`, `analyst_upgrade`, `signal_velocity`
- **hold_days** — planned hold duration in calendar days (varies by strategy)
- **stop_loss_price** — price at which to exit to limit loss (= buy_price × 0.97)
- **target_exit_date** — planned exit date (entry + hold_days business days)
- **actual_sell_date** — date position was actually closed (manual entry)
- **signals** — Technical signals that fired at entry (see Signal Definitions below)
- **status** — `OPEN` or `CLOSED`
- **exit_price** — Price at close in local currency (manual entry)

### Entry Analytics *(auto-populated when trade is added)*

- **rsi_at_entry** — RSI(14) value on entry date. 50–70 = healthy momentum zone
- **adx_at_entry** — ADX(14) on entry date. ≥22 required; ≥25 = strong trend
- **minervini_at_entry** — Minervini Trend Template score out of 8 on entry date (see below)
- **vol_ratio_entry** — Volume on entry day ÷ 20-day average volume. >1.5 = institutional interest
- **atr_ratio_entry** — ATR(14) on entry ÷ ATR(14) 20 days prior. <0.8 = coiling (useful for breakout trades)
- **market_regime_entry** — `BULL` if SPY was above its 50-day SMA on entry date, else `BEAR`. Most momentum strategies fail in BEAR regime
- **sector** — GICS sector from yfinance (e.g. `Technology`, `Industrials`). Used for sector rotation analysis

### Exit Analytics *(auto-populated when status → CLOSED)*

- **rsi_at_exit** — RSI(14) on exit date. Compare vs entry to see if momentum exhausted
- **adx_at_exit** — ADX(14) on exit date. Falling ADX = trend weakening
- **minervini_at_exit** — Minervini score on exit date. Drop from entry = structure deteriorating
- **vol_ratio_exit** — Volume ratio on exit day. High vol on exit = distribution signal

### Post-Entry Analytics *(auto-populated once 5 trading days have passed)*

- **max_dd_1wk** — Worst intraday close vs entry price in first 5 trading days (%). Used to calibrate stop-loss placement. e.g. `-3.2%` means stock fell 3.2% at its worst before recovering
- **exit_reason** — Manual annotation: `1wk_auto`, `2wk_auto`, `manual_stop`, `manual_target`, `news_exit`

## Minervini Trend Template (score /8)

Each condition = 1 point. Requires ≥6 to pass momentum scanner, ≥5 for breakout scanner.

1. Price > 150-day SMA
2. Price > 200-day SMA
3. 150-day SMA > 200-day SMA
4. 50-day SMA > 150-day SMA
5. Price > 50-day SMA
6. Price ≥ 130% of 52-week low (at least 30% off the bottom)
7. Price ≥ 75% of 52-week high (within 25% of peak)
8. 200-day SMA is higher than it was 20 trading days ago (trending up)

## Momentum Scanner Signals (`momentum_scanner.py`)

### Fresh Signals *(must fire within last 3 bars — trigger entry)*

- **MACD** — MACD line crossed above signal line
- **RSI50** — RSI(14) crossed above 50 (from below)
- **EMA921** — 9-day EMA crossed above 21-day EMA
- **P>SMA50** — Price reclaimed the 50-day SMA from below

### Confirmation Signals *(shown after `·` — strengthen the case)*

- **VOL** — Volume > 1.5× 20-day average (institutional buying)
- **ADX↑** — ADX rising (trend accelerating)
- **RSI✓** — RSI in 50–70 range (momentum zone, not overbought)
- **HIST** — MACD histogram just turned positive (momentum shift)
- **StRSI↑** — Stochastic RSI turning up from below 50 (early momentum)

---

## Breakout Scanner Signals (`breakout_scanner.py`)

### Coil Signals *(stock building the base — shown as `COIL`)*

- **ATR<** — ATR(14) < 72% of its prior value (volatility contracting)
- **VOLdry** — Volume < 65% of average (drying up = no sellers)
- **NearPiv** — Price within 4% of the pivot high (near the breakout point)
- **ADXcurl** — ADX between 10–25 and rising (trend starting to build)
- **RSIbase** — RSI between 38–62 (neither oversold nor overbought — basing)
- **RS~ok** — Relative strength vs local benchmark is neutral or positive
- **RngTght** — 10-day price range < 50% of average range (tight consolidation)

### Break Signals *(breakout firing — shown after `▶`, weighted ×2)*

- **VolSurge** — Volume > 2× average (institutional push through resistance)
- **PriceBrk** — Price closed above the 20-day high (pivot breakout)
- **ADXaccel** — ADX > 20 and rising fast (trend confirming)
- **EMAxover** — 9 EMA crossed above 21 EMA (momentum crossover on breakout day)

## NR7 Scanner Signals (`nr7_scanner.py`)

- **NR7** — Today's high-low range is the narrowest of the last 7 days (Toby Crabel compression setup)
- **VOL>avg** — Volume above 20-day average (at least some activity on compression day)
- **RSI50-70** — RSI in momentum zone (not overbought)
- **ADX>20** — Trend present (expansion likely directional)
- **M≥6** — Minervini score ≥6 (strong trend structure)
- **compression** — Stat field: `1 - (today_range / prior_6d_min_range)`. Closer to 1 = tighter compression.

## BB Squeeze Scanner Signals (`bb_squeeze_scanner.py`)

- **BB-SQZ** — Squeeze just released: Bollinger Bands were inside Keltner Channels yesterday, broke out today with positive MACD histogram
- **VOL1.5x** — Volume > 1.5× 20-day average (institutional push on release)
- **RSI>50** — RSI above 50 (momentum direction is up)
- **ADX>15** — Some trend present
- **MACD+** — MACD histogram positive (momentum confirmation)
- **M≥5** — Minervini ≥5 (minimum trend structure — relaxed for sideways markets)
- **squeeze_intensity** — `1 - (bb_width_today / bb_width_6m_min)`. Closer to 1 = tightest squeeze ever in 6 months = highest expected expansion.

## High Tight Flag Scanner Signals (`high_tight_flag_scanner.py`)

- **HTF** — High Tight Flag: stock up ≥90% in ≤40 trading days (8 weeks), then consolidating ≤25% from the pole high
- **POLE+X%** — The pole gain percentage (e.g. `POLE+120%` = stock doubled in 8 weeks)
- **FLAG-X%** — Current drawdown from the 8-week high (e.g. `FLAG-12%` = 12% off peak = tight flag)
- **VOL1.5x** — Volume above average on flag breakout attempt
- **RSI>50** — RSI above 50
- **M≥6** — Minervini ≥6 (stock must have underlying trend structure)
- **pole_return** / **flag_drawdown** — Raw stat fields in results for analysis

## Analyst Upgrade Scanner Signals (`analyst_upgrade_scanner.py`)

- **UPGRADES×N** — N distinct firms upgraded to Buy-equivalent in last 5 trading days (e.g. `UPGRADES×4`)
- **firms listed in conf** — Up to 5 firm names shown in the conf column (e.g. "Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Barclays")
- **has_tier1** — `True` if ≥1 upgrade is from a tier-1 firm (GS, MS, JPM, BofA, Citi, Barclays, UBS, etc.)
- **n_upgrades** — Raw count of distinct firms that upgraded (stat field)
- Buy-equivalent grades counted: Buy, Strong Buy, Overweight, Outperform, Accumulate, Add, Long-Term Buy
- Rejected if: stock gapped up >5% in last 3 days (earnings pile-on), or >75% analysts already rate Buy

## Signal Velocity Scanner Signals (`signal_velocity_scanner.py`)

The scanner replicates TradingView's technical summary using 15 indicators. Each gives +1 (buy), -1 (sell), 0 (neutral).

**Moving Average signals (7 total):** Close vs SMA20, SMA50, SMA100, SMA200, EMA20, EMA50, EMA200

**Oscillator signals (8 total):**
- **RSI(14):** <40 = buy, >60 = sell
- **Stochastic %K (×2):** <20 = buy, >80 = sell; %K above %D = buy direction
- **CCI(20):** <-100 = buy, >+100 = sell
- **Williams %R(14):** <-80 = buy, >-20 = sell
- **MACD histogram:** positive = buy
- **Bull Bear Power (Close − EMA13):** >0 = buy
- **Ultimate Oscillator:** >55 = buy, <45 = sell

**Net score:** sum of all 15 signals. Range: −15 (fully bearish) to +15 (fully bullish).

**Fresh signal tag:** `SV+N` where N = velocity (points gained in 3 days, e.g. `SV+8`)
- **SCORE:X/15** — Current net score shown if ≥8
- **VEL+N** — Velocity gain shown if ≥8
- **net_score** / **velocity** — Raw stat fields for analysis

**Signal fires when:** net_score ≥ 3 AND velocity ≥ 6 AND 3 consecutive days of increase AND net_score ≤ 12 (not already maxed)
